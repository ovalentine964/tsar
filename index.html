About

The story behind TSAR.

TSAR (Trading Super Agent for Returns) is a self-improving autonomous trading system that combines 13 specialized AI agents, a 7-layer deterministic risk engine, and a self-improvement flywheel that makes every trade generate proprietary knowledge.

Built 2026-08-05with Python, Rust, C++, and Solidity — running on local LLMs at $0/day. The system executes across 100+ centralized exchanges and major DEXs with MEV protection, while smart contracts on Polygon enforce trustless risk limits.

Open source under the MIT License. Contributions welcome.
