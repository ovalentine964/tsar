### The Kelly Criterion at $10

At $10, the Kelly formula suggests very small bets. But there's a problem:

```
Full Kelly = (W × R - L) / R
Where: W = win rate, R = reward/risk ratio, L = loss rate

At 55% win rate, 2:1 R:R:
Full Kelly = (0.55 × 2 - 0.45) / 2 = 0.325 = 32.5%

Half Kelly (recommended) = 16.25%
```

**But at $10, 16.25% = $1.63 risk per trade.** This is actually too aggressive for micro-capital. The problem: a 3-loss streak ($4.89 lost) puts you at $5.11 — a 49% drawdown that takes 96% gain to recover.
